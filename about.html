We help complex programmes, foundations, and purpose-driven organisations communicate their mission through high-quality animation that informs, inspires, and delivers measurable results.
From public health campaigns and education initiatives to sustainability programmes and social impact reporting, we create animation that helps organisations explain complex topics with clarity and confidence.
Our approach combines strategic thinking, world-class storytelling, and efficient production processes to turn technical information into content that audiences understand, remember, and act on.
Triggerfish Impact is the purpose-driven division of Triggerfish, leveraging the studio’s world-class storytelling and animation expertise to help NGOs, foundations, and mission-led organisations communicate their impact through compelling animated content.
